# Contributor Data Consent — TinyVision

Please complete, sign and return with your first upload. Keep a copy for
yourself.

## 1. What you are contributing

Videos (and still images derived from them) of 3D-printing sessions that you
recorded, plus the metadata you attach (printer model, camera, angle,
lighting, print result).

## 2. What you confirm

* You own or control the footage you contribute, and it does not infringe
  anyone else's rights (yours is your own printing session; no faces, no
  third-party logos as the subject, no music).
* The printer being filmed is yours, or you are allowed to film it.

## 3. Permission grant

For each line you tick you grant TinyVision and its contributors a
permission, irrevocable for the footage you submit:

* [ ] **Training**: your footage may be used to train and validate TinyVision
      models, including derivative works (e.g. models trained on it).
* [ ] **Evaluation**: your footage may be used to evaluate models and be
      reported on in the project's research outputs.
* [ ] **Redistribution**: your footage, and derivatives, may be published /
      redistributed as part of the TinyVision open dataset. If unticked, the
      footage is used for training/evaluation **only** and is never
      redistributed as-is. Note that models trained on it may still be
      released (that is what training permission is for).

## 4. No warranties

You provide footage on an "as is" basis and you acknowledge that TinyVision
teams do not verify the labels by automated means; every frame is stored as
`pending` until a human reviews it. Neither TinyVision nor any contributor
makes a warranty that the footage will be corrected or published, or a claim
about the safety or quality of your prints.

## 5. Withdrawal

You may withdraw future contributions at any time. Footage already merged into
published trained artifacts cannot be un-trained; TinyVision will, on request,
remove unreleased copies of your footage that are still under its control.
