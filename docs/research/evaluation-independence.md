## Why this matters

Environment-level splitting is TinyVision's primary defence against leakage.
A suite can look "held out" while still sharing a camera venue, lighting and
printer with training data — in which case high scores measure recognition of
one environment, not generalization. The RC1 transfer failure (normal holdout
FPR 1.000; immediate alarm on every real-world normal clip) is exactly the
symptom of single-venue overfitting that a single-venue test set cannot reveal.

## Verdict

**The present suite is NOT environment-independent.** `dev_val` and
`frozen_test` are recording-disjoint but single-venue, so they measure
within-environment generalization only. The framework's leakage check cannot
detect venue-level non-independence here because the CSV surfaces expose only
`path,label` — there is no `environment_id`/`source_id` column to compare, so
"no leakage" is a weak statement, not evidence of independence.

Items required for a genuine environment-independent test, none usable today:

1. **stage13 normal holdout** — absent (frames git-ignored, not committed).
2. **Trier FFF-QC v2** — staged metadata only; media (≈48 GB) not downloaded.
3. **Bambu timelapse** — candidate_only; timelapse cadence, not continuous.
4. **Own footage** — must collect on target hardware/cameras.

## Consequences and actions

* Treat all RC1 within-suite numbers (`val`/`test` FPR 0.091, F1 0.876,
  AUROC 0.892) as **single-venue** figures, not generalization estimates.
* Keep the `normal_holdout` (and `cross_environment`,
  `min_environments`/`min_sources`) gates **required** in the release policy;
  they are the only guard against silent transfer regressions and they must
  remain INCONCLUSIVE while their data is absent.
* To make the suite environment-independent, extract the Trier holdout
  (requires the ≈48 GB download) or collect own footage, then add a surface
  with `environment_key`/`source_key` so the leakage check has real units.
* A regression test asserts `dev_val`/`frozen_test` recording disjointness when
  the split CSVs are present (`training/tests/test_eval_independence.py`).
