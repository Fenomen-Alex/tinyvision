TinyVision — local 3D-print failure-detection demo

EXPERIMENTAL — not validated for production use. This runs a research prototype classifier over a video recording on your machine. Outputs are for inspection and software testing only; they are not a validated failure detector and may be wrong. No printer, camera, cloud account or paid API is used.
